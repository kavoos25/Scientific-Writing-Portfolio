
# Scientific Writing Portfolio

## About

This repository contains evidence-based biomedical literature reviews and educational scientific articles written by Dr. Kavoos Momeni, a Molecular Genetics and Bioinformatics researcher with extensive experience in biomedical research, scientific literature analysis, and academic publication.

## Writing Philosophy

The articles in this portfolio are designed to:

* Summarize current scientific evidence from peer-reviewed literature.
* Distinguish between preclinical, clinical, and approved-indication evidence.
* Use conservative, research-oriented language.
* Avoid promotional or unsupported medical claims.
* Present complex biomedical concepts in a format accessible to educated non-specialist readers.

## Current Articles

### Semaglutide: Pharmacology, Clinical Efficacy, and Emerging Roles in Metabolic Disease

A narrative review discussing the pharmacology, clinical efficacy, safety considerations, and emerging applications of semaglutide in obesity and type 2 diabetes management.

### Retatrutide: Current Evidence and Emerging Research on Triple Receptor Agonism in Obesity and Metabolic Disease

A review of the emerging triple agonist retatrutide, focusing on mechanism of action, clinical evidence, safety profile, and future therapeutic potential.
